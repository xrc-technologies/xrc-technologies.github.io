XRC Technologies was founded with the mission of creating high quality UAV hardware both designed and manufactured in the USA.

With over a decade of UAV experience ranging from FPV racing drones, large fixed-wings, to even smaller 'tiny-whoop' sized drones, our products are designed by pilots, for pilots. We only engineer products that we ourselves use and go to great efforts to give the highest performance gear at a reasonable cost.

Additionally, we aim to backup our performance claims with real-world testing data. No AI-general slop, no marketing  propaganda. Just the facts.
